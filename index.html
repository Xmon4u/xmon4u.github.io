Description

I'm XMON4U. Most of what I write starts with something that annoyed me: a video I couldn't save, a chat I wanted to archive, a security check I kept running by hand. I fix it, then I polish it until it's something other people can use.

My daily driver is Kali Linux. I work in Python, C++ and JavaScript, and I care about tools that are quick to start and don't get in your way.
